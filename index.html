Help & FAQ

Honest answers about what this tool can and cannot do.

You upload a video, choose clip start and end times, add captions, and export vertical 9:16 clips. All processing happens in your browser with free libraries.
Real AI needs a server or paid API, which this free version avoids. Instead you will pick moments yourself, or use a transcript you provide. We will never show fake AI results.
No. Downloading YouTube videos is not permitted by YouTube's terms and is not possible from a browser anyway. Please upload videos you own or have rights to use.
Your browser records the cropped, captioned video in real time, so a 30 second Short takes about 30 seconds. Keep the tab open and visible. Chrome, Edge and Safari can save MP4; browsers that cannot (such as Firefox) save WebM instead and the app tells you which you got.
Browser storage is far too small for video files, so projects save only your clips, captions and style. Choose the same video file again and everything is restored.
Only in your browser's LocalStorage. They are not uploaded anywhere and will be lost if you clear site data or switch browsers.
